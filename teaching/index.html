Teaching

Teaching Awards

  1. Innovation in Pedagogy and High-Impact Practices Teaching Award, Oklahoma State University, 2026

    The Spears School of Business gives this award for the best teaching in its courses that helps students learn through active participation or teamwork.

    The school gave it for Marketing Research (MKTG 4333) in Spring 2026. That semester, 210 students in two sections worked in teams on three research projects, two of them done for clients, 67 Oklahoma organizations in all. AffonsoGPT, an AI teaching assistant built for the course, answered students' questions at any hour but did not do their assignments. Students rated their confidence in conducting marketing research at 2.41 out of 5 at the start of the semester and 4.09 at the end.

  2. Engagement with Practice and Social Impact Teaching Award, Oklahoma State University, 2025

    The Spears School of Business gives this award for the best course activities that help students learn through direct work with business or nonprofit professionals.

    The school gave it for Marketing Research (MKTG 4333) in Spring 2025. That semester, 143 students in 22 teams did research for 22 Oklahoma organizations and presented their findings and recommendations to them. The organizations were 17 small businesses, 4 nonprofits (among them the American Lung Association and United Way of Central Oklahoma) and the Spears School's Marketing and Communications department. Half of the organizations said they planned to use some or most of the students' recommendations.
